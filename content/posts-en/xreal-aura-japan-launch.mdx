XREAL AURA, the Android XR glasses developed by XREAL together with Google, opened for pre-orders in Japan on November 2, 2026. This article summarizes the prices, timing and key specs reported by Japanese media and company announcements.

*This is a summary of announcements and news reports as of November 2026, not a hands-on review. Prices and dates can change.*

## What it is

XREAL AURA is a wired XR glasses product running Google's Android XR. It uses an optical see-through design, so you see your surroundings through the lenses. The glasses connect by cable to a separate compute pack, which keeps the glasses body to 95g or less.

## Price and timing in Japan (as of November 2026)

- **12GB/256GB model:** 229,800 yen (tax included)
- **16GB/512GB model:** 269,800 yen (tax included)
- **Launch price:** some reports mention a price from 199,900 yen until the end of the year
- **Pre-orders:** opened on November 2, 2026
- **Shipping:** reported as late November; some reports say November 27

Reports differ on the exact release date, so check the official store for the latest information.

## Key specs

- **OS:** Android XR (supports Google Play apps and more than 150 XR apps)
- **Processor:** Qualcomm Snapdragon Reality Elite
- **Display:** 1,920 x 1,200 per eye, up to 120Hz
- **Field of view:** up to 70 degrees
- **Tracking:** 6DoF and hand tracking
- **Audio:** Sound by Bose
- **Sizes:** M (IPD 60-66mm) and L (IPD 66-72mm)

## What you can do with it

- Place several apps in front of you while still seeing the room
- Use apps such as YouTube and Google Maps
- Watch video on a large virtual screen or work with a browser and documents
- Connect a compatible PC by USB-C and adjust the PC screen's size and position
- Ask Gemini about what you see or the video you are watching

## Points to consider

- **Wired design:** the cable between the glasses and compute pack needs managing.
- **Reported visual issues:** people who tried it at Google I/O were reported to point out that the real world looks somewhat dark through the lenses and that the edges of the view can blur.
- **Price:** it costs more than 200,000 yen, so it helps to decide what you will use it for first.
- **Sizing:** choose M or L according to your interpupillary distance.

## Also announced: XREAL 1 Pro+

XREAL also announced pre-orders in Japan for the XREAL 1 Pro+ AR glasses, reported to release on November 10 at 84,980 yen for the glasses alone, or 98,960 yen in a bundle with XREAL Eye. It is a successor to XREAL's display-style AR glasses rather than an Android XR standalone device like AURA.
